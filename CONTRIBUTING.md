# Contributing to SCRTH - Privacy Policy & TOS

Thank you for being part of the team and helping us maintain our legal and community guidelines. This public repository hosts the source code and text for the official SCRTH Terms of Service and Privacy Policy website. 

As a staff member, your contributions are essential to keeping this information accurate, transparent, and compliant.

## 👥 Roles & Permissions
* **System tester** : You are welcome to propose updates, fix typos, or draft new policy sections by opening a [PR](https://github.com/SCRTH-Developement-Management-team/scrth-tos/pulls)
* **Supervisors+** : You are welcome to propose updates, fix typos, or draft new policy sections by opening a [ticket](https://discord.com/channels/1421603145074737254/1421763128852615219).

## 📝 Critical Guidelines
1. **Clear and Universal Language:** Legal and community terms must remain simple, accessible, and easy to understand for all community members.
2. **Update Timestamps:** Every time a modification is made to the TOS or Privacy Policy, you **must** update the `Last update: DD/MM/YYYY` line at the top of the main document to ensure accurate version history.
3. **Data Privacy Strict Rule:** This is a public repository. **Never commit or push any personal user data** (such as real application forms, Discord IDs logs, or private decisions) to this repository. 

## 🚀 How to Contribute

### 1. Create a Branch
Never make direct edits to the production branch. 
* Always branch off from the main branch using a descriptive name:
  `git checkout -b feature/update-tos-october` or `fix/typo-contact-section`

### 2. Make Your Changes
* Edit the relevant markdown or HTML files.
* Ensure your formatting matches the existing structure of the page (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Contact sections).

### 3. Open a [Pull Request](https://github.com/SCRTH-Developement-Management-team/scrth-tos/pulls)
Once your changes are pushed to GitHub:
1. Open a **Pull Request** targeting the main branch.
2. Provide a clear, concise title (e.g., *“Update Section 2 regarding data storage guidelines”*).
3. In the description, briefly explain **why** this policy or text update is required based on management or organizational decisions.

### 4. Review & Merge
* Wait for the innovation director to review your draft.
* Address any requested changes directly on your branch. Once approved, a director will merge the PR to update the live website.
