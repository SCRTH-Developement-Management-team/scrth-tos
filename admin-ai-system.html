How it works

Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite Discord-native No payout needed

The Admin AI system currently runs on Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite. It calls the Gemini API directly from Discord, so there's no separate app, dashboard, or subscription involved on your end.

For general information about the underlying model itself, head to Google's own support pages.

Data handling

We do not store any of your information in any of our databases through this system.

The only information we do store is for SV+ members who explicitly accepted to be listed, so that the bot can answer questions other members ask about that specific person. Nothing is stored without that opt-in.

If you have any concerns, open a ticket and ask for it to be transferred from the SV team to the ID (Innovation Department).
