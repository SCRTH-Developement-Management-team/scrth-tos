Become a Developer

The role, internal contract, and steps to join the development team.

Who are the current developers in SCRTH?

The current dev is deadslights, his job is mainly to manage the bots and most of the internal systems around SCRTH. You're free to contact him by a ticket if you have any inquiries.

What does a developer do?

The developer builds and maintains the technical tools used by SCRTH, including Discord bots and systems that support applications and team operations.

The work may include adding features, fixing bugs, and improving existing tools in coordination with the Innovation team.

How to become a developer

1. Open a ticket

Open a ticket in the Discord server's Bug Report section to request an internal development contract.

Open a Discord ticket

2. Provide the contract information

Clearly state that you are requesting an internal developer contract and provide the information requested by the team in the ticket. You can also include your experience, skills, and availability to help staff review your request.

3. Complete the trial period

The trial period lasts two weeks. If it goes well, you will then be offered a developer contract for three months.

4. Renew the contract

At the end of the term, the three-month contract can be renewed. There is no limit on the number of renewals.
